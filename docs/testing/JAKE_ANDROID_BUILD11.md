# Jake's Android retest — Aptly Able 0.1.2, build 11

The updated website, backend and Android download are live.

## Install the update on your phone

1. Open https://plaud.aptlyable.info/enroll on your Android phone.
2. Tap **Download for Android**. Open the completed `aptly-able-android.apk`
   download and install it as an update. Downloading alone does not install it.
3. Open Aptly Able from its phone icon. In Settings, confirm **0.1.2 · Build 11**.
   Do not uninstall the existing app, release its assignment or reset the recorder
   just to update.

If Android blocks the installation, use the help on the download page or send
Zach the exact message. If it says the installed app conflicts with this update,
stop and send that message instead of uninstalling.

## Set up and connect

1. Open **Set up my recorder** or the **Recorder** tab. Sign in with the same
   account used previously. You do not need a computer or QR code.
2. Your saved recorder should be found through your account. Continue with it if
   prompted. If none is saved, choose **Plaud NotePin S**, enter its complete
   serial number and tap **Save recorder**.
3. Keep the charged recorder beside the phone. Briefly press its button to wake
   it and look for the white light. Keep Bluetooth and internet access available.
4. Follow the recorder-permission prompts, search for your recorder, and connect
   the assigned recorder. Wait for **Connected and ready**.
5. If it is still paired to the Plaud app or another account, it needs to be
   unpaired there with the recorder present before pairing in Aptly Able. Do not
   unpair a working Aptly Able connection simply to install the update.

Once connected, make a short, non-sensitive test recording, stop it, and check
that it transfers into Recordings and plays back. Note any unclear steps.

## What is easier now

- Download and setup can both start on the phone; QR invitations are optional.
- Signing in finds recorders already assigned to that account.
- Model and serial entry are available directly inside the app.
- Incomplete or mismatched serials receive feedback before saving.
- Setup goes directly to the recorder connection screen, with recovery for an
  unfinished setup and safe retries.
- Connection progress identifies more specific stages, and Settings can copy a
  diagnostic report if secure setup fails.

## If secure setup fails again

The original Bluetooth secure-connection failure has not yet been proven fixed
on your hardware. This build makes setup simpler and provides better information
to diagnose a remaining failure.

Immediately open **Settings → Copy setup details**, before retrying, signing out
or closing the app. Send Zach that report and the exact error message. Also say
whether it failed with a message or remained spinning for over a minute.

The report excludes credentials, serial numbers, recording content and location.
Do not send your password or private recordings.
